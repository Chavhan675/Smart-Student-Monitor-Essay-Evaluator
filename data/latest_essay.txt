Technology has changed the way students learn and communicate.
Students can use online platforms to access educational resources
and learn new skills. Artificial intelligence can also help students
understand difficult topics.

However, students should not completely depend on technology.
They should develop their own thinking and problem solving skills.
Regular reading, writing and discussion can improve communication.

In my opinion, technology is useful when students use it properly.
The future of education will depend on a balance between technology
and human thinking.
